Start the recorder, keep the phone with you, and let it write down what a website is allowed to know about your day: how you move, how the phone is held, magnetic field, light, battery, network, screen state. Everything stays in this browser. When you are done, export two CSV files: a per-second sample table and a process-mining event log (case_id, activity, timestamp).
this is the same data a tracking SDK collects in the background. The difference: you hold the file. Open the event log and look at what it says about when you sat, walked, charged, slept and picked the phone up. Browsers freeze sensors when the screen locks, so keep the recorder in the foreground with the screen on (a charger helps). Every gap is logged as an event of its own.

▶ what is in the files?

samples.csv: one row per sample interval. Motion channels are aggregated over the interval (mean of gravity-including acceleration, mean/rms/max of linear acceleration, mean rotation rate), orientation and magnetometer are the last reading, plus light, battery, charging, network type, screen orientation, tab visibility and the optional GPS and sound level. Empty cells mean the sensor does not exist on this device or browser.

events.csv: state changes only, ready for process mining. Columns: case_id (local day), activity, timestamp (UTC, ISO 8601), session_id, detail. Activities are activity: still | handling | moving, pose: flat face up | flat face down | upright | landscape | tilted, screen: visible | hidden, power: charging | discharging, network: online | offline, light: dark | dim | bright, recording: started | stopped | gap | resumed | interrupted. A new state is only emitted after it has held for three samples, and its timestamp is the first sample of that state.

The activity labels come from simple thresholds on motion magnitude. They are a heuristic and vary by device, so check them against your own day before trusting them.

Pose signs follow Android. On iOS the accelerometer axes are inverted in some versions, so "face up" and "face down" can be swapped.
